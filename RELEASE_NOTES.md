## What's new in v3.8.0

- **Timing meter**: a bar at the bottom of the screen in levels, like a rhythm game. Every click leaves a mark showing how many frames early it was (green: saved, blue: a training hint, orange: not saved because of the save rate), on time (the middle) or a click nothing could save (red, on the right). Turn it on in the new **Display** tab.
- **Save map**: green marks on the progress bar wherever the buffer saved you in the level, next to the red death heatmap.
- **Save rate per mode**: the **Frames** tab has a new **Frames / Save rate** switch, so cube, ship, wave and the rest can each have their own save rate.
- **Floating button**: a Saturn button over the level that pauses and opens the menu. Drag it anywhere and it stays there; tapping it never makes you jump. On by default on phones.
- **Profiles**: in the **Tools** tab, save up to 6 setups under a name, load them in one tap, and share them as a code (`KOSMOS1:...`) that friends can import from their clipboard.
- **Report bug**: also in **Tools**. It copies the log and opens a GitHub issue with the details already filled in; just paste the log.
- The menu has a new **Display** tab for everything you see and hear while playing, and the Tools tab now has room for its buttons.

## Install

Put Kosmos.geode in your Geode mods folder and remove older copies (InputBuffer.geode, TESTEBUFFER.geode or basu.input-buffer.geode), or both run at once. On iOS, upload it with the Geode launcher's web upload (see the [website](https://porqueijjmzm1.github.io/Kosmos/#install)).

Based on Click Before Frames by BASU.
